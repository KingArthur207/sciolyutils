    Why drill this?

    Patristocrats hide the word breaks, columnar and other transpositions scramble the letters outright, and every cryptogram ends with "what word has these letters?". Fast anagramming is the difference between a guess and a solve.

    How to get fast

    • Pull the vowels out first, then try common endings: -ED, -ER, -ING, -S.
    • Look for pairs that travel together: TH, CH, ST, QU.
    • When a set wants several words, the first one you find usually hints at the rest: swap two letters.

    Link a preset round: /anagram/?len=6&t=120 or /anagram/?mode=any&t=180. The same word pool powers the upcoming pattern-matching drills, so scores stay comparable across tools.
