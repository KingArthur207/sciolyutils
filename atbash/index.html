Why drill this?

Atbash shows up on its own and hidden inside other questions. There is no key to find, so the whole question is recall speed: if the pairs are automatic, it is the fastest points on the test.

The pairs

Thirteen pairs, and each works both ways. The middle ones trip people up: M ↔ N, L ↔ O, K ↔ P.

AZ BY CX DW EV FU GT HS IR JQ KP LO MN
