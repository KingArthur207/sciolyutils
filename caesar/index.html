Why drill this?

Every shift cipher on the test, Caesar, Vigenère, Porta and the Affine's additive step, comes down to moving a letter a fixed distance and wrapping at Z. Doing that without counting on your fingers is what makes brute-forcing 25 shifts feasible.

How to get fast

  • Big shifts go the other way: +19 is −7, +22 is −4.
  • Jump by landmarks: +13 flips A↔N, +5 lands on the next vowel-ish row.
  • Weak spots are reported per key, so drill the shifts that slow you down.
