vigenere

Vigenère from the Division A rules: decrypt a letter when you are told the key. Key E, cipher R: count back 4 from R and you get N. Start with plain letters and the table, or turn on the numbers and let the math do the work.

The math

  • Give every letter its number, A = 0 up to Z = 25.
  • To decrypt: cipher − key. R is 17, E is 4, 17 − 4 = 13, and 13 is N.
  • If you go below 0, add 26. B 1 − E 4 = −3, −3 + 26 = 23, which is X.

The table way

  • Find the key letter down the left side. That is your row.
  • Slide along that row until you hit the cipher letter.
  • Look straight up to the top: that column's letter is the plain letter.

For coaches

Division A, DaVinci Decoder: decryption given the key. Suggested ladder: /vigenere/?help=steps, then ?help=numbers, then ?help=none&key=word. Weak spots are reported per key letter, so you can see which shifts still need the table.
