Why drill this?

Affine, Hill and every mod-26 step lean on instant letter ↔ number recall. Every second spent counting on your fingers is a second not spent solving.

How to get fast

  • Anchor the landmarks first: A0 · E4 · J9 · O14 · T19 · Z25.
  • Run the strip for a day, then turn it off.
  • Do one direction until it is clean, then mix.
