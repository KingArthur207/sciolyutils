Directions

Turn on one or both. With both on, prompts alternate at random.

Table

Science Olympiad tests use the classic 24-letter table, where I and J share ABAAA and U and V share BAABB. Either letter is accepted for those codes.

Duration

Codes take longer to type than numbers, so 90 or 120 seconds is a fair round.

Extras

You can type 0 and 1 instead of A and B. After three misses on one prompt the answer is revealed; it does not score.

Why drill this?

Baconian questions hide the A/B pattern in fonts, cases or word lengths. Once you see the groups of five, the decode is pure recall. Knowing the table cold turns a ten-minute question into a two-minute one.

How to get fast

  • It is binary: A = 0, B = 1, and the code is the letter's position counted from A = 0.
  • Learn the row starts: A AAAAA, I ABAAA, R BAAAA.
  • In the 24-letter table everything after I shifts up one, and again after U.
