Symbols

Fractionated Morse writes x between letters and xx between words; that part is the cipher, not the code, so it is not drilled here.

Duration

Anything from 5 to 3600 seconds works.

Extras

Keys: . or , for a dot, - or _ for a dash. After three misses on one prompt the answer is revealed; it does not score.

Why drill this?

Fractionated Morse, Morbit and Pollux all start and end with Morse. The table is printed on the test, but every glance at it is a context switch. Fluent Morse lets you read the plaintext as the triplets fall out.

How to get fast

  • Learn by length: E T · I A N M · S U R W D K G O · then the fours.
  • Pairs that mirror: A •– / N –•, D –•• / U ••–, B –••• / V •••–.
  • Do Morse → letter first; that is the direction decoding needs.
