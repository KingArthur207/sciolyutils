How to use it

Two minutes a day beats an hour once a week.

Pick a drill

Start with one direction and the reference strip on. Turn the strip off as soon as it feels like cheating.

Set a timer

Sixty seconds is plenty. Short rounds keep you honest and make it easy to run one before practice starts.

Beat your best

Bests are saved per setting on this device. Check the weak-spot report at the end and drill those letters next.

Coaching a team?

Link a preset round straight from your practice plan and have students paste their summaries into the chat. New drill ideas are welcome.
