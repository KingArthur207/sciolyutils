Why drill this?

Tap Code is the one Division A cipher where the table is not on the resource sheet. You either rebuild it from scratch under the clock or you know it. Kids who know it read the dots straight off.

How to remember the table

  • Five rows of five, alphabetical, with K squeezed into C's cell. Row starters: A F L Q V.
  • First group of taps is the row, second is the column. ● ●●● is row 1, column 3: C or K.
  • A test shows all the groups in a line; mark off pairs as you go so you never lose your place.

For coaches
