Why drill this?

The Standard Galactic Alphabet is a straight substitution: one symbol per letter, table provided. Speed comes entirely from recognition, so a few minutes a day here turns a slow lookup question into a quick one.

How to remember them

  • Dots are your friend: R is four dots, W is three, J is a dotted line, I is a broken line.
  • Look-alikes come in pairs: B and V, H and T, N and X. Learn the pair, not the symbol.
  • Do the 12 most common letters first; they cover most of any sentence.
