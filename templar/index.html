Why drill this?

On the test you get the table, but looking up every symbol is slow and easy to mix up. Kids who know the common letters by heart finish the Knights Templar question with time to spare.

How the cross works

  • Four arms, always in the order top, right, bottom, left.
  • Open angles: A B C D. Triangles: E F G H. Kites: I/J K L M. The X in the middle is N.
  • Add a dot to go round again: O P Q R, S T U V, then X Y W Z (X on top, W at the bottom).
